Supported serial layouts

20 digits beginning 7716: FD digits 11–13, within the checked January 2005–June 2010 layout window. 23 digits beginning 5570: FD digits 5–7, within the checked January 2010–December 2029 window.

The manufacturer FAQ’s July 2010 boundary wording conflicts with its explicitly dated June 2010 example; that conflict is retained. A date code does not identify the model, fuel or GC number. Manufacture date is separate from installation date and guarantee expiry.

Full-serial dates use the checked layout and matrix with moderate confidence, except the manufacturer-dated example. FD-only dates are table references until the serial layout and appliance applicability are established. No code is extrapolated to another decade.

Sources and confidence

The existing checked matrix is reused unchanged. General Bosch tables and third-party cross-checks are distinguished from Worcester boiler-specific manufacturer guidance. Table dates alone do not establish applicability to every boiler.

Serial number or badge photo

Enter the full boiler serial, or take a close, readable photo of its data badge. Check every character before using a photo reading.

Supported now: Ideal’s separate YYWW barcode code with the 2000-onwards era checked, Baxi’s matching 14-character serial layout, or the separate manufacture code on a Viessmann Vitodens 100-W Heat Only B1GA. Older or different formats may need manufacturer confirmation.

Find the separate four-digit group immediately after the serial number, under the barcode: serial → YYWW. Use this option only when you can confirm the sticker is from 2000 onwards. You do not need to know the exact year first. If the era or sticker layout is uncertain, choose the older/different option.

Ideal’s confirmed example: 2501 → 2025, week 01. Older Classic, Mini and other different formats remain on hold. Do not take four digits from inside the serial.

Read a badge photo JPEG or PNG, up to 12 MB. Crop closely to one badge. The reader runs on this device; the first reader download needs a connection. No photo or serial is uploaded.
